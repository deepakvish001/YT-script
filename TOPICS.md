## Pehle ye 3 banao

### 1. Hormuz band → India ka kya hoga? 🔥💰
**Title ideas:** "33 KM का वो रास्ता जो India का पेट्रोल रोक सकता है" / "अगर Hormuz हमेशा के लिए बंद हो जाए?"
- **Kyun abhi:** 2026 mein US–Iran takraav ke baad Hormuz lagbhag band chal raha hai (Oct 2026 ki reports), July mein ek Indian seafarer ki maut hui.
- **Scene:** 1991 — India ke paas sirf ~3 hafte ke import ka forex, Gulf War se tel mehenga, aur raat mein India ka **47 tonne sona hawai jahaz se London** bheja gaya.
- **Domino:** tel → diesel → truck bhaada → sabzi/doodh mehenga → LPG cylinder → khaad (fertilizer) → agli fasal.
- **Proofs:** 1973 Arab oil embargo · 1991 India BoP crisis.
- **Hook number:** duniya ka ~20% tel is 33 km chaude raaste se guzarta hai (EIA).
- **Verify:** India ke crude/LPG import ka kitna % Hormuz se aata hai; strait ki latest status.
- ⚠️ Chalu conflict hai — kisi side ka propaganda nahi, sirf verified facts.

### 2. Super El Niño — wo mausam jisne India mein ~1 करोड़ log maar diye 🔥♾️
**Title ideas:** "समंदर का वो बुखार जो India को भूखा मार सकता है" / "El Niño: 1877 का अकाल फिर आ सकता है?"

- **Scene:** 1 Jan 1877, Delhi — Lord Lytton ka shahi Durbar, hafte bhar ki daawat; usi waqt Madras presidency mein log bhookh se mar rahe the.
- **Domino:** Pacific garam → hawaayein palti → monsoon kamzor → fasal fail → anaaj export jaari → akaal.
  (Ye bilkul nuclear-winter wala chain hai — style ke liye perfect match.)
- **Proofs:** 1876–78 Great Famine (5.5 se 1 करोड़+ maut, **verify**) · 2015–16 El Niño drought.
- **Ant:** aaj India ke paas FCI buffer stock hai — umeed + chetavani dono.

## Natural disaster series ♾️

### 4. Toba — wo volcano jisne insaano ko lagbhag khatam kar diya
- ~74,000 saal pehle Sumatra; uski **raakh aaj bhi India ki mitti mein** (Jwalapuram, Andhra Pradesh).
- Twist: Jwalapuram ke auzaar dikhate hain ki **India ke insaan shayad bach gaye the** — bottleneck theory par bahas dono taraf dikhao.
- Ant: Yellowstone / agla supervolcano.

### 5. Solar storm — agar kal sooraj ne internet band kar diya 💰
- **Scene:** 1 Sept 1859, Carrington event — telegraph ke kaagaz mein aag, Caribbean tak aurora.
- **Proofs:** 1989 Quebec blackout (9 ghante) · Feb 2022 Starlink ke ~40 satellite gire · May 2024 — **Ladakh (Hanle) mein aurora dikha**.
- India hook: Aditya-L1. Domino: GPS → UPI/banking → bijli grid → paani ke pump.

### 6. Asteroid — Tunguska 1908 se 2032 tak
- **Scene:** 30 June 1908, Siberia, 8 करोड़ ped ek pal mein zameen par.
- Aaj: 2024 YR4 (Chand se takraane ka ~4% chance, Dec 2032 — **verify, JWST data se update ho sakta hai**), Apophis 13 April 2029.
- NASA DART (2022) — "insaan ne pehli baar asteroid ka raasta badla".

## Future threats 💰

### 10. Superbugs — wo din jab ek chhoti chot bhi jaan le legi
- **Scene:** 3 Sept 1928, Alexander Fleming chhutti se laute, petri dish par fungus.
- Fleming ki 1945 Nobel speech mein khud warning thi.
- India hook: superbug **NDM-1 ka naam "New Delhi"** par pada. Lancet (2024): 2025–2050 mein ~3.9 करोड़ maut.

### 11. China ka "Water Bomb" — Medog dam 🔥
- 19 July 2025 se nirmaan, ~$167 bn, Arunachal se ~30–50 km. Duniya ka sabse bada hydro project.
- **Scene:** 15 Aug 1950 — Independence Day ki raat, isi ilaake mein 8.6 ka bhookamp (Assam–Tibet earthquake).
- **Proof:** June 2000 Siang flash flood (Tibet mein landslide-dam toota, China ne data nahi diya).
- Saath mein: Indus Waters Treaty "abeyance" (April 2025) → "paani ki jung".

- Domino: UPI → stock market → IT exports.

### 13. Kessler Syndrome — space ka kachra
